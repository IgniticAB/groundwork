# Source essays

Two essays written in May 2026 that the `foundation/` files were derived from.
They are kept for provenance, not as current guidance.

**Read `skills/groundwork/foundation/` instead.** Where these essays and the
foundation files disagree, the foundation files are right and these are the
record of what was thought at the time. Several specifics in them were checked
against vendor documentation in November 2026 and did not survive: per-trigger
word budgets for Cursor rules, character limits for Windsurf rules, a
`.windsurfmemories` repo file, and a `requiresConfirmation` field in MCP. None
of those exist. They are left in place here rather than quietly corrected,
because an essay edited to agree with its own conclusions is not provenance.

They are also visibly LLM-assisted, down to the "Works cited" sections. That is
what they are.
